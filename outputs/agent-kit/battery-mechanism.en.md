# How the AM Infinity .97 battery swap system works

Prepared revision; not published.

The AM Infinity .97 uses a hot-swappable magnetic battery system. Keep one battery in the mouse while the spare charges on the receiver, then swap in the charged spare when needed.

## Two batteries, two roles

The captured product page lists two batteries and a receiver with battery charging. One battery powers the mouse; the receiver charges the spare.

## What the swap changes

Swapping installs a battery that has already been charged. It does not recharge the battery inside the mouse during the swap. This system keeps a charged spare available for repeated use.

## Understanding battery life

A single battery still needs recharging. Battery life depends on settings and use. This revision provides no charging-time, reconnection-time or runtime guarantee.
